how we won geesehacks with learnetf

so in january 2025 me and my team did geesehacks, its a hackathon at waterloo. we picked the sun life challenge which was about helping gen z actually understand investing. we had 2 days

we built learnetf. its basically gamified lessons that teach you what ETFs are and how they work, kind of like duolingo but for investing. you go through short lessons and quizes and earn progress as you go

stack was react, typescript and tailwind. we kept it simple cause 2 days isnt alot of time

hardest part honestly was making finance stuff not boring. we rewrote the lesson content like 3 times to make it shorter and less jargony. also getting the quiz flow to feel smooth took way longer then expected

we ended up winning the sun life challenge which was crazy, first hackathon win for me

biggest takeaway: scope small and polish the demo. judges care way more about a clean demo then a bunch of half done features
